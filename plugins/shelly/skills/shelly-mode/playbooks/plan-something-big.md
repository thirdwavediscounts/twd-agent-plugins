### Plan something big

The user drives the interview skills; the mode does the groundwork.

1. `/shelly:how` over the affected subsystems and `/shelly:why` for the decisions that shaped them; put both in the reply as the brief.
2. Offer `/shelly:grill-with-docs` (or `/shelly:wayfinder` when questions keep chaining) → `/shelly:to-spec` → `/shelly:to-tickets`.
3. Once tickets exist, write the throughput checkpoint (blocking first steps, independent workstreams, shared state, smallest decomposition) and offer `/shelly:team` or `/shelly:work`.
